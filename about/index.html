ABOUT GASEL COOKING

We cook the way we grew up eating.

Simple food, good ingredients, hands-on cooking and people around the table. Meet Edoardo and Gaia, the hosts bringing Italian home cooking to kitchens across Luxembourg.

Explore the Experience

OUR STORY

From Italy to Luxembourg.

We are Edoardo and Gaia. We created GASEL Cooking to share the kind of Italian cooking we grew up with: simple, hands-on and meant to be enjoyed together.

Cooking has always been more than following a recipe for us. It is about knowing your ingredients, feeling the dough with your hands, understanding a few essential techniques and having the confidence to make something delicious yourself.

When we moved to Luxembourg, we wanted to bring that atmosphere with us. Rather than opening a restaurant or setting up a formal cooking school, we decided to do something more personal: travel directly to people's homes, bring the ingredients and utensils, and cook together in their own kitchen.

MEET YOUR HOSTS

Two people, one love for Italian food.

GASEL started from something very simple: we love cooking at home, and we love sharing that table with other people.

EDOARDO · CO-FOUNDER & HOST

Italian roots, lots of pasta.

Cooking has always been part of everyday life for Edoardo. From long family meals in Italy to experimenting with regional pasta shapes and sauces, food has always been his way of bringing people together.

During our masterclasses, Edoardo loves breaking down traditional pasta techniques into simple, practical steps so everyone around the table feels comfortable working the dough by hand.

GAIA · CO-FOUNDER & HOST

Curiosity, baking and a love for good food.

Gaia brings the same warmth and curiosity to the kitchen, with a particular love for baking, artisanal focaccia, sourdough bread and the small details that make homemade food feel special.

She makes sure every class feels welcoming, unhurried and personal, guiding guests through flavors, textures and recipes they can genuinely recreate on their own.

OUR APPROACH

We don't cook for you. We cook with you.

Our masterclasses are not demonstrations where you stand back and watch someone else work. You get your hands floury, ask questions, taste along the way and learn by doing.

We adapt the pace and the menu to the people around the table. Whether it is your first time making fresh pasta or you already cook every evening, we make the experience relaxed and rewarding.

01

Hands-on from start to finish

From mixing the flour and eggs to shaping the pasta and finishing the sauce, you make every part of the meal yourself.

02

Tailored to your group

We choose the appetizer and fresh pasta dish together after booking, adapting to your tastes, dietary needs and occasion.

03

We bring the ingredients & utensils

We arrive with the ingredients and pasta tools. You only provide a functional kitchen with a stovetop, oven, basic cookware and plates.

04

Made for sharing

Once the cooking is complete, we tidy up our workspace and leave you to sit down and enjoy the meal together at your own pace.

WHY YOUR HOME

The best place to learn is your own kitchen.

There is something special about cooking in a familiar space. You already know where everything is, you can relax with friends or family without feeling rushed, and the techniques you learn are immediately connected to the kitchen you actually use every day.

When you see how simple it is to roll fresh pasta on your own countertop, it becomes a skill you keep long after the evening ends.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

“Italian cooking does not need to be complicated. Good ingredients, a few techniques, some patience and people to share the table with. That is usually enough.”

Edoardo & Gaia
