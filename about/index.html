ABOUT GASEL

We cook the way we grew up eating.

Simple food, good ingredients, hands-on cooking and people around the table. That is what GASEL Cooking is all about.

OUR STORY

From Italy to Luxembourg.

We are Edoardo and Gaia. We created GASEL Cooking to share the kind of Italian cooking we grew up with: simple, hands-on and meant to be enjoyed together.

Cooking has always been more than following a recipe for us. It is about knowing your ingredients, understanding a few basic techniques and having the confidence to make something yourself.

When we moved to Luxembourg, we wanted to bring that feeling with us. Rather than opening a restaurant or creating a traditional cooking school, we decided to do something more personal: come to people's homes and cook together.

EDOARDO & GAIA

Two people, one love for Italian food.

GASEL started from something very simple: we love cooking, and we love sharing it with other people.

EDOARDO

Italian roots, lots of pasta.

Cooking has always been part of everyday life for Edoardo. From family meals to experimenting in the kitchen, food has always been a way of bringing people together.

GAIA

Curiosity, baking and a love for good food.

OUR APPROACH

We don't cook for you. We cook with you.

Our masterclasses are not demonstrations where you watch someone else cook. You get your hands dirty, ask questions, make mistakes and learn along the way.

We adapt the pace and the recipes to the people around the table. Whether you are completely new to fresh pasta or already spend a lot of time in the kitchen, there is always something to learn.

WHY YOUR HOME

The best place to learn is often your own kitchen.

There is something different about cooking in a familiar space. You already know where everything is, you can ask questions without feeling intimidated, and the techniques you learn are immediately connected to the kitchen you use every day.

So we bring the ingredients, utensils and preparation to you. All you need is a functional kitchen and a little curiosity.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Italian cooking does not need to be complicated.

Good ingredients, a few techniques, some patience and people to share the table with. That is usually enough.

Edoardo & Gaia
