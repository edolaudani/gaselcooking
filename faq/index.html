FAQ

Before we cook together.

Everything you need to know about the experience, from booking and menus to your kitchen and the day itself.

HAVE A QUESTION?

Here are the things people ask us most.

If you cannot find what you are looking for, just get in touch. We are happy to help.

01

Booking

From choosing a date to confirming your experience.

How do I book a Masterclass? +

You can request a Masterclass directly through our website or contact us by email. Once we confirm the date and details with you, the experience is reserved for your group.

When is my booking confirmed? +

Your booking is confirmed once we have agreed on the date and the details of your experience. We will send you the relevant confirmation information afterwards.

Can I reschedule or cancel my booking? +

Yes. Rescheduling and cancellation are possible subject to our cancellation policy. If you have already booked, please use the links provided in your booking confirmation email or contact us directly.

Can I book a Masterclass as a gift? +

Absolutely. We offer gift vouchers for the Masterclass. The recipient can then contact us to arrange a date that works for them.

02

The experience

What happens when we arrive at your kitchen?

Is the Masterclass suitable for beginners? +

Yes. You do not need previous cooking experience. We guide you step by step and adapt the pace and recipes to your group. More experienced cooks are welcome too.

Do we actually cook, or do we just watch? +

You cook. The Masterclass is designed to be hands-on, so you will prepare the food alongside us rather than simply watching a demonstration.

What will we cook? +

The Masterclass focuses on handmade fresh pasta. The menu is defined together after booking and normally includes an Italian appetizer and a fresh pasta dish, with the recipes adapted to your preferences and experience level.

How long does the experience last? +

The Masterclass typically lasts around 2 to 2.5 hours. This gives us enough time to prepare the dishes together without rushing the experience.

Can we eat the food we prepare? +

Of course. Once the cooking is finished, everyone sits down together to enjoy the meal you have prepared.

03

Your kitchen

We bring what you need. You provide the kitchen.

Do I need to buy ingredients? +

No. We bring all the ingredients needed for the Masterclass.

Do I need to have cooking equipment? +

We bring the kitchen utensils and preparation equipment needed for the experience. You simply need a functional kitchen with a stovetop and oven.

What does my kitchen need? +

A normal, functional kitchen with a stovetop and oven is enough. We also need a reasonable amount of working space so everyone can take part comfortably.

Do I need to prepare anything before you arrive? +

Not much. We bring the ingredients and utensils and take care of the preparation. We only ask that the kitchen and working surfaces are available when we arrive.

04

Food & menu

Italian cooking adapted to the people around the table.

Can the menu be adapted to our preferences? +

Yes. After booking, we discuss the menu with you and adapt the recipes to your preferences, experience level and the type of experience you are looking for.

Can you accommodate vegetarian or vegan diets? +

Yes. Please let us know about dietary requirements or preferences when booking so we can plan the menu accordingly.

Can you accommodate allergies? +

Please tell us about any allergies or intolerances before the experience. We will discuss the menu with you and determine what can be safely adapted.

Can we request a specific pasta shape or recipe? +

Usually, yes. We have a range of fresh pasta shapes and recipes and can discuss what would work best for your group when planning the menu.

05

Groups & events

From an evening with friends to larger celebrations.

How many people can participate? +

Our standard Masterclass is designed for 2 people or small groups of 3–8 people. We can also organise larger private, corporate and special events with a format adapted to the group.

Plan a Private Event →
Can you organise corporate or team events? +

Yes. We can adapt the format, menu and logistics for corporate groups and team events. For larger groups, get in touch with us so we can discuss the right setup.

Can you organise birthdays or special occasions? +

Absolutely. The experience can be adapted to birthdays, anniversaries, dinner parties and other celebrations. Tell us about the occasion when you book and we can adapt the experience around it.

Can you accommodate groups larger than 8? +

Yes. Larger groups are possible, but the format depends on the number of guests, the available space and the kitchen setup. Contact us and we can work out the best arrangement for your event.

06

Gift vouchers

A cooking experience is a pretty good present.

Do you offer gift vouchers? +

Yes. We offer gift vouchers for our private at-home cooking experiences. They are available for couples, friends, family and special occasions.

How does a gift voucher work? +

You request the voucher, we prepare it for you to give to the recipient, and they then contact us to arrange the date of their Masterclass.
