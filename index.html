HOW IT WORKS

The kitchen comes to you.

No cooking school. No crowded demonstration. Just you, your kitchen and an Italian meal made together.

01

Choose your experience

Pick a masterclass or tell us what you would like to cook. We can adapt the menu to your group and dietary needs.

02

We come to you

We arrive at your home with the ingredients, utensils and everything needed for the class.

03

Cook together

We prepare everything step by step, explaining the techniques and small details that make Italian cooking work.

04

Sit down and enjoy

Once everything is ready, sit down together and enjoy what you have made.

MORE THAN A RECIPE

Learn by
actually cooking.

We do not stand on the other side of the kitchen and demonstrate. You make the dough. You shape the pasta. You taste the sauce. You adjust it.

The aim is not just to make one good meal. It is to leave the kitchen feeling more confident about making it again yourself.
