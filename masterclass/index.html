The experience

Italian cooking,
made together.

We bring everything you need to your home and guide you through the preparation of a traditional Italian meal, from start to finish.

The masterclass is designed to be relaxed, practical and social. You will learn how to make fresh pasta from scratch, prepare its sauce and put together a few simple Italian dishes along the way.

No previous cooking experience is needed. We take care of the ingredients and utensils, while you simply need a functional kitchen with some working space and basic pots, pans, plates and cutlery.

01

We come to you

The entire experience takes place in your home.

02

Flour, eggs, vegetables, cheese and everything else you need.

03

You cook with us

Hands-on preparation, from the first ingredient to the finished dish.
