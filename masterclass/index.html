A private, hands-on Italian cooking experience at your home. Learn how to make fresh pasta from scratch and cook a complete Italian menu together, step by step.

ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

Learn by actually cooking.

The Masterclass is a hands-on Italian cooking experience for anyone who loves learning by doing. We come directly to your home and cook step by step together, guiding you through a complete Italian menu, from fresh pasta dough to the final dish.

It is a relaxed and interactive experience where you cook, ask questions, taste along the way, and truly understand Italian home cooking.

After booking, the menu is defined together and usually includes one appetizer and one handmade pasta dish, adapted to your tastes, dietary requirements and experience level.

AT YOUR HOME

Italian cooking, in your own kitchen.

We bring the ingredients and kitchen utensils. You only need a functional kitchen with a stovetop and oven.
